# Wallpaper credits

Files 02a, 02b, 03-06 and 08-10 are public domain or CC0 (sources below). Each was cropped to 16:9 and resized to 1920x1080 (JPEG); no other edits were made. License statements are quoted from the source page (or from the policy page the source site applies to its images) as checked on 2026-11-11.

Files 01a and 01b are original artwork drawn for Vitro by a procedural script (`wallpapers-src/gerar/ondas.py`), with no third-party image as a starting point. Files 02a and 02b are public domain photos of the Dumont Dunes (Mojave Desert, California) taken in daylight by the US Bureau of Land Management, recolored into a moonlit night scene by `wallpapers-src/gerar/noite.py` (sky replaced with a night gradient and faint stars, sand mapped to blue tones, 02a trimmed 7% at the bottom), then cropped to 16:9 and resized to 1920x1080. `wallpapers-src/gerar/gerar.sh` rebuilds all four. Checked on 2026-11-16.
